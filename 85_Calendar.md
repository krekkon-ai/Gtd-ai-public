# Calendar

The Calendar contains **time-specific commitments and actions** that must happen on a particular date or at a particular time.

The Calendar is part of the GTD system, but it is **not a general task list**. Tasks that can be done at any suitable time belong in `01_NextActions.md` or on the relevant project page.

## What Belongs on the Calendar

Put an item on the Calendar when the timing is genuinely specific, for example:

- appointments and meetings
- events with a fixed start time
- deadlines that must be met on a particular date
- actions that can only be done during a specific time window
- time-specific preparation or follow-up

Examples:

- Doctor appointment — Tuesday, 10:30
- Submit application — Friday, before 17:00
- Call the office — Monday, 09:00–12:00

## What Does Not Belong on the Calendar

Do **not** put ordinary tasks on the Calendar merely because they would be nice to complete on a certain day.

For example, a task such as `Clean the garage` should remain a Next Action unless there is a real time-specific commitment attached to it.

The Calendar should represent the **hard landscape** of the user's commitments, not become a list of intentions.

## Calendar Processing Rules

### Add an item

An item should be added here when:

1. it has a genuine date or time requirement, and
2. missing the date or time would make the action inappropriate, late, or impossible.

When the timing is not genuinely specific, keep the item in the appropriate GTD list instead.

### Review an item

For each Calendar item, check:

- Is the date still correct?
- Is the time still correct?
- Is the commitment still active?
- Does preparation need to happen beforehand?
- Does a resulting action belong in Next Actions, Waiting For, or a project?
- Has the event or deadline already passed?

### Completed or passed items

Completed Calendar items should not remain as active commitments.

If the item produced an actionable follow-up, process that follow-up into the correct part of the GTD system.

If appropriate, the completed action can also be retained in `99_Done.md` so that completion and actual time can be tracked consistently.

## Relationship to Other GTD Lists

- `00_Inbox.md` → unprocessed inputs waiting to be clarified.
- `01_NextActions.md` → actions that can be done when the appropriate context, time, and energy are available.
- `02_Routines.md` → recurring routines and regular responsibilities.
- `80_WaitingFor.md` → things delegated or dependent on another person or organization.
- `85_Calendar.md` → genuinely date- or time-specific commitments.
- `Someday/` → ideas and possibilities that are not currently committed.
- `99_Done.md` → completed work and actual-time records.

## Weekly Review

During the Weekly Review, review the Calendar with the upcoming weeks in view.

Check for:

- upcoming appointments and commitments
- deadlines
- preparation actions that need to be completed before an event
- overdue or missed commitments
- actions that should move from Calendar into another GTD list after the time-specific trigger has passed

Only genuinely time-specific actions should be moved to the Calendar. Do not use it as a substitute for the Next Actions list.

## Core Principle

The Calendar answers the question:

> **What must happen on a specific day or at a specific time?**

Everything else should live in the GTD category that best represents what it is and what needs to happen next.
