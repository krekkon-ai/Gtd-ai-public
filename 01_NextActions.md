## Usage

A Next Action is a concrete, physical action that can actually be performed as the next step of a task or project.

Priority and estimated time help with selection, but they do not replace the GTD considerations of **context + available time + energy**.

Project-specific Next Actions remain on their respective project pages. This file contains standalone Next Actions.

For completed Next Actions, the actual time is recorded in `99_Done.md` during `/done` processing.
